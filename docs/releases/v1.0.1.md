# Leverage 1.0.1

Better AI meeting notes, for Listen meetings and Studio recordings alike, and a fix for summaries failing on Groq.

## Install

Download `leverage-1.0.1-setup.exe` and run it. It upgrades 1.0.0 in place and keeps your settings, keys, recordings and notes. Quit Leverage from the tray first.

The installer still isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **Notes grouped by topic.** The AI summary now has a Summary, then a **Topics** section with one heading per subject in the order discussed, then Decisions, Action items and a new **Open questions** section.
- **More detail for longer meetings.** Short calls stay brief. Meetings over about 10 minutes get a fuller summary and more bullets per topic, and those over about 40 minutes keep the reasons, options and objections behind each point.
- **Keeps the specifics.** Names, figures, dates and tools that were said are kept. Who said something is named only when the transcript makes it clear.
- **Stronger reasoning on Groq.** With Groq's gpt-oss models, notes are written with high reasoning effort. They take a little longer and come out more thorough. Dictation cleanup stays fast.

## Fixed

- **"Summary failed: Groq: Failed to validate JSON."** Notes are now returned as plain Markdown instead of inside JSON. A single escaping slip by the model used to make Groq reject the whole summary.
