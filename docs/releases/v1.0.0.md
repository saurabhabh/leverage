# Leverage 1.0.0

The first release of Leverage, a free Windows app with three tools in one: **Studio** (screen recording and editing), **Snap** (screenshots and annotation) and **Listen** (dictation and meeting notes).

## Install

Download `leverage-1.0.0-setup.exe` and run it. It installs for your user only, with no admin rights needed, and adds a desktop shortcut.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Studio — record, edit, share

- Record a screen, a window or a dragged region, with the microphone, the PC's sound and a movable camera bubble. Each one is saved as its own track.
- **Ctrl+Shift+R** starts or stops a recording, **Ctrl+Shift+P** pauses it and **Ctrl+Shift+X** throws it away. A small toolbar shows while recording.
- Edit on a timeline: split, cut, reorder and undo, plus blur boxes, text callouts and lower thirds.
- Transcripts, made on your PC with Whisper (a one-time download) or through Groq. Delete words in the transcript to cut them from the video, remove pauses and filler words in one click, or ask AI (Groq or OpenRouter) to suggest cuts.
- Export to MP4 or GIF.

## Snap — screenshots

- **Ctrl+Shift+2** freezes the screen. Pick a region or the full screen, with an optional delay.
- Annotate with a pen, marker, lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses, text, numbered steps, pixelate and redact, and crop. Undo and redo work, and edits save automatically.
- Copy, save as, pin a shot on top of other windows, or browse all shots in the gallery.

## Listen — dictation and notes

- **Ctrl+Shift+Space** in any app starts dictating, and the text is pasted where you were typing. Your clipboard is restored afterwards. **Esc** cancels.
- Optional AI cleanup, a custom dictionary, and a history with Retry for failed dictations.
- Meeting notes record your microphone and the PC's sound, then add a transcript and an AI summary with key points, decisions and action items. You can also make notes from any Studio recording.

## Good to know

- Everything is stored on your PC: recordings and screenshots in your library folders, settings and API keys in your Windows profile (keys are encrypted).
- Groq and OpenRouter are optional. Add your own keys in Settings to use cloud transcription and the AI features.
